About

MithraVoice turns spoken words into live, on-screen captions — transcribed and translated in real time, right as someone is speaking. It's built for rooms where people follow along in more than one language at once: presentations, lectures, meetings, and panels.

What it does

Speak naturally — no scripts or special pauses needed. Your words appear as text within moments, with the translation displayed right alongside, sized and styled for whoever's reading it rather than just the presenter.

Built for the room

Show the original and translation together or just one, pin captions to the top/bottom/center to fit your slides, scale the font from a small huddle to the back of a lecture hall, and match your theme with Light, Dark, or Custom appearance.

Languages

English, Persian (Farsi), Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Mandarin Chinese, Turkish and Dutch (Netherlands) — any language to any other, in both directions. Pick the pair with the From/To boxes on the Live Translation page.
